# applied thinking
### Theory, Research & Practice in Program Management


Applied Thinking is a collection of case studies documenting my work in program management, strategy, research, and evaluation. My approach draws on interdisciplinary research and theoretical frameworks to understand organizational challenges, design practical interventions, and evaluate how those approaches work in real settings.

I examine how organizations use knowledge, make decisions, and translate ideas into practice. Each case study explores a problem, the approach I developed, its implementation, and what the evidence reveals.
